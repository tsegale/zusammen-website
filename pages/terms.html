4. Cancellations and Refunds

Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to info@zusammentravels.com. The following cancellation policy applies:

  • More than 60 days before departure: Full refund minus deposit and administration fee

We strongly recommend that all clients obtain comprehensive travel insurance, including cancellation cover, prior to travel.
