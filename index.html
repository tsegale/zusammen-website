About Zusammen Tours and Travel Agency

A Single Platform for the Entire Journey

Zusammen Tours and Travel Agency makes every journey simple, enjoyable and perfectly tailored to you. We specialise in luxury fly-in safaris and more, delivering unforgettable adventures with comfort, style and a sense of excitement at every turn.

We focus on travel within Namibia while also offering destinations across Africa. Our clients, mainly from Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom and Germany, rely on us to guide their travel journeys.

We provide a range of services from immigration advice to study-abroad support and vacation planning.
